How to choose a website builder

The easiest builder on day one is not always the most suitable option after your site grows. Start by defining whether you need a brochure site, a blog, a sales funnel or a custom web app.

Questions worth asking

  1. Can you use a custom domain and control metadata?
  2. Can you export content or move to another provider?
  3. Does it support the forms, payments or email tools you require?
  4. What are the recurring fees, transaction fees and usage limits?
  5. How does it handle accessibility, security and backups?

Match the tool to the job

Marketing funnels and custom applications have different needs. Test a small example before committing to a paid plan, and check the latest provider terms directly.
