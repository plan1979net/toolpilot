AI automation: how to choose a tool

Before paying for an AI agent, define one repetitive task with a clear starting point and an observable result. Examples include categorizing incoming requests, drafting a first response or summarizing structured notes.

Start with the workflow, not the product

  1. Write down the input, the desired output and who approves it.
  2. Check whether the tool connects to the apps you actually use.
  3. Test with non-sensitive sample data first.
  4. Measure time saved and the frequency of mistakes.
  5. Review pricing, usage limits and cancellation terms on the official website.

AI agents versus simple automation

A fixed, predictable sequence may be easier to maintain with conventional automation. An AI agent can be useful when inputs vary, but it also needs supervision and clear limits.
